The problem

The right features, but hard to use. The current product feels unfinished and tiring.

UI audit: seven findings

I reviewed the live website and app screen by screen and grouped the problems into seven findings.

1Web + AppIcon inconsistency

Thick and thin outlines, filled and outline styles mixed, and icons that don't match their meaning.

2Web + AppLogo used as the search icon

The search field shows the Majlis logo instead of a magnifier, so it doesn't read as search.

3WebSidebar collapse hides content

Collapsing the sidebar removes custom feeds, drafts and my communities completely.

4WebExtra scrollbar in latest posts

The latest posts panel scrolls inside itself, creating two scrollbars and trapping the mouse wheel.

5AppCreate post needs work

Wrong button label, a Post button that works when empty, and the bottom menu visible while writing.

6Web + AppFlat font hierarchy

Almost all text is dark and bold, so nothing stands out and every line has to be read.

7Web + AppWeak overall layout

No clear focus, posts start low on the page, and mixed container styles and spacing.

Process and principles

From audit to redesign in five steps, guided by five principles.

  1. ExploreUse the live website and app as a real user.
  2. AuditList every UI and UX issue and group them into findings.
  3. DefineSet design principles from the findings.
  4. RedesignRebuild the homepage for web and mobile.
  5. ReviewCheck the new design against each finding.
Content firstOne icon languageClear hierarchyNothing hidden or trappedOne product on web and app

A small visual system

Arabic type with a clear hierarchy, a calm green palette and one icon language.

Typeface: Noto Sans Arabic · Regular, Semibold, Bold. Icons: one outline set, one stroke weight, filled only when active.

Website redesign

A calm homepage that puts posts first. Every change answers a finding.

  1. Search that looks like searchA magnifier replaces the logo; the logo stays in the corner.Fixes finding 2
  2. Sidebar with your communitiesCustom feeds, drafts and my communities are always one click away.Fixes finding 3
  3. Latest posts, no inner scrollA fixed list of posts, so only the page scrolls.Fixes finding 4
  4. Clear hierarchy on every postBold titles, grey meta and body, white cards with a vote column.Fixes finding 6, 7
  5. One outline icon setSame stroke and style everywhere; filled only when active.Fixes finding 1

Mobile redesign

The same system on the app homepage, from top to bottom.

  1. Logo back in the top barThe brand is visible on the first screen.
  2. Real search fieldFull width, with a magnifier, right under the header.
  3. Suggested communities as cardsAvatar, name and one clear Join button.
  4. Calm post cardsVotes, comments and share in one row.
  5. Bottom bar with CreateFive tabs, with Create raised in the middle.

About

Designer with a requirements analyst’s habits

I’m Muhammad Ammad, a UI/UX designer with more than eight years of experience across the UK, USA, UAE and Pakistan. I started out in graphic and frontend work, moved into UX, and now spend most of my time on design systems and product UI.

Lately my focus has been UK mobile brands (MVNOs) like Talk Home Mobile, Now Mobile and RWG Mobile Wales. Telecom sites look simple but carry a lot of detail: plans, roaming, top-ups, terms. That’s where a clear brief and a solid system pay off.

I don’t stop at Figma. I can build production-ready frontend components myself, and I use AI tools to speed up the repetitive parts of design while reviewing every output by hand.
